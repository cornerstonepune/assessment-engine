The day

Every weekday runs on the same bells. Core learning in the morning, application after lunch, two breaks.

Fluid levels need one rule. Maths and R1 sit at the same hour for every section of a grade, so children can move between level groups without clashing with anything else.

The school's proposal (what is designed, not official)
  • Calendar: Monday to Friday, a 7-week summer break, a 3-week Diwali break and 12 weekday holidays. These are typical of Indian schools; the school's real calendar replaces them.
  • Day: 8:30–2:30 with a 20-minute circle, a 15-minute snack break, a 30-minute lunch and a 10-minute close. That leaves 285 teaching minutes, or 4¾ hours.
  • The block plan and the weekly grids on this page. Grades 1–2 take NCF-SE's daily minutes for R1, R2 and Maths exactly.
  • Longer days go to the afternoon studio. Saturday hours go to clubs, sport, arts and making.
  • Which of the eight capabilities each block mainly builds.
  • R1 is counted as English, since only English outcomes are imported. The R1/R2/R3 choice is still the founders' decision.
Not checked, or not yet counted
