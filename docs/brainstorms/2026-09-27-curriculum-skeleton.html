One outcome card · Science · Grade 2 · Forces

Pushes and pulls change how things move and their shape

illustrative card for this brainstorm · the engine would draft it
Rests on these official lines
2Pf.01 Know that forces can change the movement of an object.Cambridge Primary Science 0097 framework (published Sept 2020), Stage 2, PDF p.17
2Pf.02 Know that forces can change the shape of an object.same, p.17
2Pf.03 Recognise that things will only speed up, slow down or change direction when something else causes them to do so.same, p.17
NCF-SE C-7.2 Observes and understands cause and effect relationships in nature by forming simple hypotheses and uses observations to explain their hypothesesNCF-SE 2023, Foundational Stage, curricular goal CG-7
NCERT sets no science outcome for Class 2: "in classes III to V, it is introduced as a separate curricular area and in I and II, the related concerns are integrated with language and mathematics." NCERT, Learning Outcomes at the Elementary Stage (2017), EVS introduction, PDF p.93
Needs first · Stage 1
1Pf.01 Explore, talk about and describe the movement of familiar objects.Cambridge 0097, PDF p.15
1Pf.02 Describe pushes and pulls as forces.Cambridge 0097, PDF p.15
Leads to · Stage 3
3Pf.01 Know that forces can be measured with a forcemeter.Cambridge 0097, PDF p.19
The school's articulation generated · not validated
Outcome. Shows that a push or a pull can start, stop, speed up, slow down or turn a moving object, and can change an object's shape. 2Pf.01 2Pf.02 2Pf.03
Base. Pushes and pulls familiar objects and says what changed: it moved, stopped, turned or changed shape. 1Pf.02 2Pf.01 2Pf.02
Advanced. Says what a push will do before trying it, then says whether the result matched. 2TWSp.02 2TWSa.01

Each clause carries the code it rests on. 2TWSp.02 reads "Make predictions about what they think will happen." 2TWSa.01 reads "Describe what happened during an enquiry and if it matched their predictions." (Cambridge 0097, p.16)

Three activity options generated · school-designed

1 · Ramp race

Do
Roll toy cars down a ramp; push softer, harder; put a block in the way.
Materials
4 ramps (boards or books), toy cars, blocks, chalk
Logistics
groups of 4 · 30 min · floor space
Observe for
says "push" or "pull" 1Pf.02; predicts before pushing 2TWSp.02; notices the car turns only when something acts on it 2Pf.03

2 · Dough press

Do
Press, pull and twist dough; draw it before and after.
Materials
dough, mats, drawing sheet
Observe for
says the push or pull changed the shape 2Pf.02

3 · Trolley and rope, outdoors

Do
Push a loaded trolley, pull a rope; which needs a bigger push?
Observe for
says what made it start, stop or turn 2Pf.01 2Pf.03

The validator, part of the backend

code checks every time

  • Every cited code exists.
  • Its text matches the official document on its page, word for word.
  • Every official line for the grade is cited, or marked "not taught" with a reason.
  • Nothing is planned before what it needs.
  • The plan fits the timetable.
  • Every mark line names an outcome and a level.

second model flags only

  • A different prompt from the one that drafts.
  • Does any clause say more than its cited lines?
  • Is any clause uncited?
  • It never approves. Its flags go to a person.

person signs

  • The subject's curriculum lead clears each flag and signs the outcome.
  • Nothing reaches an educator unsigned.
  • Trace: any mark in a child's record, to the question, to the outcome, to the official line and page.

Found while checking

  • Everything imported so far is verbatim. NCF-SE 2023: 814 of 814 lines found in the document. NCERT outcomes, Classes 1–10: 729 of 729 found on their stated page. Cambridge Primary Science: 297 of 297 objectives found in the framework.
  • Not yet in: Cambridge Primary English, Global Perspectives and Computing; the current Cambridge Primary Maths; Cambridge Lower Secondary; IGCSE; the CBSE curriculum for Classes 9–10 (downloaded, not imported). The Maths framework on disk uses an older code style (2Nc6), so it is probably a pre-2020 edition.
  • The Cambridge Science copy came from a school website that hosts Cambridge's PDF. The official copy sits behind your Cambridge support-site login; the system should use that one.
  • Your Grade 2 science units teach Cambridge Stage 3. Forcemeters (3Pf.01), gravity (3Pf.02), friction (3Pf.03), shadows (3Ps.02), food chains (3Be.01) and solids, liquids and gases (3Cm.01) are all Stage 3 lines. This is a keyword match; a person should confirm it.
  • For Grade 2 science, Cambridge is the only grade-level source. NCERT has none for Classes 1–2, and NCF-SE sets competencies per stage, not per grade.
