# The teacher's flow — a sketch for after the engine and its structure (not built)

Brainstormed with Nimish 2026-09-26/27. Built only after the engine elements and the structure (BUILD-ORDER).

**What is wrong today.** The menu (Today, Children, Marking, Make papers, Curriculum) follows the engine's own four
workflows, not how an educator thinks: "what needs me today, how is my class, what did this child get wrong, what do
I print on Monday".
- A paper has no home: made under Make papers, its worksheet under Curriculum, its scan under Marking.
- Approvals sit in three places: skill sets (Curriculum), packs (Make papers), answers (Marking).
- Curriculum hides skills, the question bank and worksheets behind one word.
- The engine's words reach the screen: capture, skill set, rung, copy03.
- There is no "my class this week" default.

**The shape.** Class → child → paper → question, and one pile of what waits for a person.

| Menu | Answers | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| This week | what needs me | one inbox: papers to approve for print, answers to check (count, minutes), skills to sign off; the class at a glance |
| My class | how is the class | children × skills, coloured by standing; a child opens |
| Child | what about this child | a timeline of every paper, score and named mistake; the skill graph; the next paper suggested |
| Papers | what did we hand out, where is it | every paper, filtered by class/week/status; each one's life: planned → printed → scanned → checked → scored; the scan drops here |
| Library | find a question or worksheet | one search; topic → level → worksheets → questions |

**Nimish, 2026-09-27:** the home assessment is its own generation, and says so in the page's title.

**Before any of it is built:** one educator does three tasks on the current site (print Monday's papers, find what a
child got wrong on 24 Sep, check today's answers) and where they stall is written down. Phone or laptop for checking
is decided then.
