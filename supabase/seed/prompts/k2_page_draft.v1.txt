Fill only the parts listed above; every other part is the empty string "".
- objective: one or two sentences, what the child gains, in the school's style.
- steps: what the educator does, step by step, as short lines; only what the activity's name, its materials and the examples support.
- thinking: how the educator should think about the idea: the idea in one sentence, then the slips children commonly make and how to see them.
- level_0 to level_3: what the educator sees. Level 0 is the child who does not sit for the class or take part; levels 1, 2 and 3 climb from watching and naming, to doing as shown, to doing it in the child's own way. Four different sentences, each about this activity.
If the activity's name and the school's own objective disagree (for example in numbers), say so in "flags" and draft nothing that depends on the disputed words.
Give "confidence" between 0 and 1 for the whole page, and in "reason" one sentence on what you relied on.
