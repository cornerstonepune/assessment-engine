For each page, look for these faults, and only these:
- a step uses a thing (a material, a tool, a number of things, a place) that the school's materials text does not name;
- a step, a slip or a level states a fact about the activity that the school's words do not support, or contradicts the school's name, objective or levels;
- the objective, steps, slips or levels are about a different activity than the one named;
- the four levels say the same thing, or level 0 is not the child who does not take part;
- a slip is not a mistake a child of this age could make in this activity.
Answer once for every page, with its "key" copied exactly. "verdict" is "agree" when you found none of these faults, otherwise "disagree". Under "faults" give one object for each fault: "part" (objective, steps, thinking, level_0, level_1, level_2 or level_3; use thinking for the idea and the slips) and "reason" (one short sentence, naming the exact words at fault). A page you agree with has an empty list of faults. Judge each page alone.

Answer with exactly the fields named above (key, verdict, faults) and no other field.
